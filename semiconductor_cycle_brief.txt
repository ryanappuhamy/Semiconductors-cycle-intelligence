# Semiconductor Cycle Brief – Q4 2025

**As of:** December 31, 2025  
**Cycle Phase:** Mid Cycle (Index: 0.743)

---

## Current Phase Assessment

The semiconductor sector has transitioned into **Mid Cycle** territory with a cycle index of 0.743, marking a strong sequential improvement from Q3 2025's Downturn reading (-0.198). The quarter-over-quarter directional momentum of 0.941 signals robust cyclical acceleration. This represents a significant inflection from the volatile Q4 2024 through Q3 2025 period, which saw three phase transitions across just four quarters.

The current Mid Cycle designation indicates demand normalization and inventory digestion completion, though the sector has not yet entered potential overheating territory (Late Cycle).

---

## Key Indicator Readings

**Equipment Revenue Growth:** +2.12σ (strongly elevated)  
- WFE spending is significantly above historical mean, confirming genuine capex recovery and capacity expansion
- This is the strongest leading indicator in the composite

**Inventory-to-Revenue Ratio:** -1.19σ (lean inventories)  
- Channel inventories are 1.2 standard deviations below normal
- Supports pricing power and reduces risk of destocking headwinds
- Validates transition from correction phase to expansion

**SOXX Relative Momentum:** +0.85σ (outperforming)  
- Sector exhibiting solid relative strength vs. broader market
- Confirms market participants pricing in recovery thesis

---

## Historical Context

The recent phase history reveals unusual volatility:
- **Q4 2024:** Downturn (-1.041) – cycle trough
- **Q1 2025:** Early Cycle recovery (-0.564)
- **Q2 2025:** Brief Mid Cycle touch (0.407)
- **Q3 2025:** Unexpected reversion to Downturn (-0.198)
- **Q4 2025:** Current Mid Cycle (0.743) – strongest reading in available history

This whipsaw pattern from Q2-Q4 2025 suggests either measurement volatility or a false start followed by genuine recovery. The current 0.743 reading is significantly higher than Q2's 0.407, suggesting more sustainable momentum this time.

**Critical Data Limitation:** Mid Cycle forward return samples are extremely thin (n=1-3 observations), creating low statistical confidence in historical benchmarks compared to Downturn (n=12-13) and Early Cycle (n=10) phases.

---

## Key Risks

1. **Statistical Noise in Phase Classification:** The Q2→Q3→Q4 2025 sequence (Mid→Down→Mid) raises questions about indicator stability or whether macro crosscurrents are creating false signals

2. **Limited Mid Cycle Precedent:** Only 1-3 historical observations for Mid Cycle returns significantly undermines predictive confidence. The single 12-month observation shows -25% to -35% returns, though this is statistically meaningless

3. **Late Cycle Transition Risk:** Equipment spending at +2.1σ suggests potential overinvestment. Historical cycles show Mid→Late→Downturn progression; current strength could age quickly

4. **Macro Divergence:** If the 2025 phase volatility reflects structural market changes (AI-driven investment patterns, geopolitical supply chain shifts), historical return distributions may not apply

---

## 6-12 Month Outlook

**Base Case (60% probability):** Cycle continues maturing through H1 2026, transitioning to Late Cycle by mid-year. Equipment spending normalizes from current elevated levels. Sector remains constructive but returns moderate as easiest gains captured.

**Bull Case (25% probability):** AI/HPC demand sustainably extends cycle duration. Current Mid Cycle phase persists through 2026 with inventory remaining lean and pricing favorable. Would represent structural departure from historical 2-3 year cycles.

**Bear Case (15% probability):** Q3 2025 Downturn reading was accurate signal; Q4 improvement proves temporary. Equipment spending at +2σ represents peak, leading to rapid Late Cycle→Downturn transition by Q2 2026.

**Expected 6-month horizon:** Modest positive returns (mid-single digits to low-teens) as momentum sustains but valuation multiples compress from cycle-peak levels.
